Profile

I'm Metong Atteng — a Civil Engineer working as a Project Engineer at Pinemax Energy, where I run planning, scheduling and cost control on utility-scale solar civil works: an integrated Primavera P6 schedule, a Work Breakdown Structure built from scratch, take-off cost estimation, a daily/weekly Excel-based cost monitoring framework tracking planned value, earned value and actual cost, extension of time claims.

I started out at the Project Office, Ministry of Works, Ondo State, supervising the dualization and construction of a 8.1 km flexible carriageway highway build (among others) — Marking-out, demolition and compensation, subgrade compaction, base course, asphaltic surfacing, and the quantity take-offs that fed interim payment certificates. That's where the interest in schedules, sequencing and site documentation actually started.

I hold a PMP from the International Institute of Project and Safety Management and I'm completing my Masters in Engineering Management, building toward a senior planning, scheduling and cost-control role in Nigeria's oil & gas and EPCC sector.
